Practice

One suite for the site and the phone

Playwright and Selenium for the browser. Appium for iOS and Android. Shared fixtures, parallel runs, and the same idea of a check on a page and on a device. It can stay on a laptop, run across regions, or go out as an experiment. A layout change comes back as a picture diff, and a person decides whether that picture should change. Models help choose what to cover and how to write what broke. The failure still has to be something you can read and rerun.

Three of the apps above came out of that work. PlayGen records a session in Chrome and writes a Playwright spec. Emulyn stands up Android emulators and iOS simulators on this Mac and answers over a local API. Loupe puts a Figma frame beside the page as it actually renders.
